Story Graph

A local-first consistency checker for novelists

Local-first · Ollama-powered

Parses a manuscript into a structured story graph and automatically catches continuity errors — a character who dies and reappears, an age that jumps years between chapters, an object referenced after it was given away, a travel time that quietly shrinks — without the author manually maintaining a story bible.

  • Small local models, not a cloud API. Relationship extraction runs entirely on local Ollama models (qwen2.5:3b) instead of offloading to a hosted AI provider — unpublished manuscripts never leave the author's machine, and it works offline.
  • Deterministic core, AI for the gaps. A dependency-parse engine resolves ownership, death, age, and travel contradictions outright; the local LLM only fills in relationship types no syntax rule can reach, like "her brother Tomas."
  • Explainable, correctable AI output. Every AI-derived fact links back to its source passage with a confidence badge, and can be corrected or dismissed — corrections persist through reprocessing instead of being silently overwritten.
  • Benchmarked for real hardware. Compared qwen3:8b, qwen2.5:3b, and qwen2.5:0.5b head-to-head on CPU-only hardware to find the actual speed/quality sweet spot, rather than assuming bigger is better.
  • Live incremental reprocessing. A file watcher re-analyzes only the paragraphs just edited and updates the Contradiction Report in real time.
  • Full visualization suite. Relationship graph, family tree, appearance heatmap, timeline, and map views, built with React, TypeScript, and Cytoscape.js.

OpenStatus

An RPG where real habits level up your character

iOS · SwiftUI

OpenStatus is an iOS RPG built on real life: finishing a workout, a reading session, or a focus block earns XP across three character aspects, levels up a persistent hero, and unlocks classes, gear, and a full adventure world to explore. It's genuinely social too — a Firebase-backed guild system lets up to 20 players cooperatively fight a shared raid boss, fueled in part by real HealthKit step counts.

  • Real habits drive real combat stats. Ten interdependent RPG stats feed 10+ combat formulas — attack power, dodge, crit, HP — derived directly from tasks like workouts, journaling, and reading, not cosmetic skins on a to-do list.
  • Guild sync without trusting device clocks. A monotonic version counter, not timestamps, resolves conflicts between devices, with the player profile split across four independently-versioned Firestore documents to stay under the 1MB doc limit.
  • HealthKit and EventKit, done correctly. Step counts fuel guild-boss attacks and achievements; recurring quests mirror one-way into the native Calendar app.
  • Barcode scanning that means something. Scanning a book's ISBN via VisionKit/AVFoundation pulls metadata from the Open Library API and, once read and rated, awards real XP — connecting a physical action to in-game progress.
  • A streak system designed against guilt. A 2–3 day "welcome back" check-in lets a broken streak continue instead of punishing a missed day — an explicit, documented anti-shame design decision.
